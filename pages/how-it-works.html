PROCESS ARCHITECTURE

How SmartCampus Operates

A structured, secure, and privacy-preserving workflow built specifically for students and staff across the MITS Gwalior campus.

END-TO-END FLOW

The Four-Stage Recovery Lifecycle

Every report moves through a controlled lifecycle from submission to verified campus return.

Stage 01 · Publication

Categorized Report Creation

Students log the item with designated campus classrooms, seminar halls, or labs (e.g. Classroom 113A J115, Seminar Hall SH-12, CIoT Lab M007) and date. Photos and descriptions are publicly listed, while identifying secrets are withheld.

Stage 02 · Matching

Structured Heuristic Matching

When a lost report is filed, SmartCampus immediately compares active found entries within the same category and campus vicinity, presenting immediate candidate matches with 1-click review.

Stage 03 · Verification

Two-Party Ownership Challenge

Claimants must submit private proof that only the legitimate owner would know — such as sticker positioning, engravings, lock wallpaper, or serial digits — effectively eliminating fraudulent claims.

Stage 04 · Return

Coordinated Campus Handover

Once proof is verified, the item is reclaimed at standard campus handover desks (Library Circulation Desk or Campus Security Office) with verified student ID sign-off.

THE UPGRADE

Traditional Notice Groups vs. SmartCampus

Why WhatsApp broadcasts fail and how a structured campus portal fixes it.

❌ The Old Broadcast Method

  • ⚠️ Messages get instantly drowned in busy class WhatsApp groups.
  • ⚠️ Anyone can falsely claim an item without any ownership verification.
  • ⚠️ No searchability — finding an item lost 3 days ago is nearly impossible.
  • ⚠️ Sensitive student details and phone numbers are broadcast publicly.

✓ The SmartCampus Framework

  • ✨ Permanent, searchable directory organized by campus location & date.
  • ✨ Mandatory private challenge questions eliminate false claims.
  • ✨ Accurate campus room tagging across Classrooms, Seminar Halls, and Labs.
  • ✨ Official institute authentication ensures campus accountability.
