STREAMLINED PROCESS

How SmartCampus Works

Four transparent steps ensure lost items return to their rightful owners safely.

01

Submit Details

Report the lost or found item with exact landmark, date, category and identifiable photo.

02

Smart Match

Our algorithms compare item types and campus locations to suggest instant candidate matches.

03

Ownership Check

Claimants answer private identifying questions (stickers, serials, lock codes) to prevent false claims.

04

Safe Handover

Items are handed over at verified campus desks (Library desk, Department office, or Security).

SECURITY GUARANTEES
